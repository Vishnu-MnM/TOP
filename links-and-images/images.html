Images

This page covers common image formats, but you may also be looking for links and useful extras.

JPGs

JPGs are good for photos.

GIFs

GIFs are good for animations.

PNGs

PNGs are good for diagrams and icons.

SVGs

SVGs are amazing. Use them wherever you can. One potential issue is that we need to convert any text fields to outlines. If there is too much text, this can have big impact on the file size.
